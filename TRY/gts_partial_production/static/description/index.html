GTS Partial Production

This module allows partial production from MO order and
also update the stock the same time

Geo Technosoft

Features

This module allows to record partial production

Updates the stock the same time , also update the stock of raw materials / produced goods

No need to wait to complete full manufacturing order to update stock

Screenshots

Here is the flow how it works
Bill of material for Computer Desk product

Now we are creating Manufacturing order of 5 quantities for Computer Desk

After checking availability and once raw material is available
'Do Partial' button becomes visible

Now we are partially producing 3 quantities out of 5(To be produced)

Here we can see respective raw materials for the 3 qty of
Computer Desk are consumed and reduced from stock.

Here we can see 3 qty of Computer Desk are produced and
increased in stock.

Now we are partially producing 2 quantities

Now all raw materials are consumed and reduced from stock

Now all(5) qantities of Computer Desk are produced and
increased in stock.

Now we have to mark related workorder as done.

Now we have to mark MO Order as done.
