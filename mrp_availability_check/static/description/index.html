BOM AVAILABILITY CHECK

 

The purpose of the “Bom Availability Check” tool is to check the feasibility of a production quantity based on the projected stock availability at all level of the Bill of Materials.

 

The tool consists of two main parts:

·       BOM explosion

·       Availability Check

 

 

Input data


·       Finished product

·       Its bill of materials

·       The production warehouse

·       Requested production quantity

 

Outcome:


BOM Explosion section

BOM is exploded till the final level and the requested quantity for all the components is calculated.

For each component, its main manufacturing attributes are provided:

·       Product Type, to distinguish between storable products and consumables and serviced

·       Make product, so its procurement is achieved internally by performing a production order

·       Buy product, so a purchase order has to be created for its procurement

·       Make to Order logistic model, so a requirement triggers its procurement

·       Reorder point replenishment model, so the procurement is triggers when its stock falls below a defined threshold

·       Manufacturing lead time from the product master data

·       Purchase lead time from the vendor pricelist: a hierarchical sequence is taken into account to select the proper supplier pricelist, i.e. the pricelist for product variant and validity period are selected with the highest priority

 

Availability Check Result section

All components requirements are summarized, i.e. components in different BoM level are listed in only one row with all its requirements summarized. The storable products are reported only.

For each item the following stock quantities are provided for the selected location (its children are taken into account also):

·       Requested Quantity based on BOM explosion

·       On hand Quantity

·       Available Quantity, i.e. the projected stock quantity

·       Incoming Quantity, i.e. all incoming goods movement in the selected location are summarized

·       Outgoing Quantity, i.e. all issue goods movements from the selected location are taken into account

·       Forecast Quantity, achieved as available quantity minus requested quantity

 

Two printouts are available also:

·       BoM Explosion report

·       Availability Check report
